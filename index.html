PROJECT BRIEF / 001
AI WORKFLOWS / AI-GNITION 2026

RippleFix.

What happens when a booked room becomes unavailable? RippleFix explores how to recover a schedule while keeping constraints, explanations and human approval in the loop.

The challenge

Room disruptions ripple across a timetable. A replacement must have enough capacity, avoid conflicts and respect unavailable rooms.

Technical approach

Constraint-aware room recommendations, scoring and rejection reasons, cascading changes and a supervised approval workflow connected to Google Calendar.
